Serbia Work Permit & Visa Pathway

Serbia is receiving special attention because of its electronic immigration system, employer-led applications and comparatively streamlined single residence and work permit procedure.

Available roles Construction, tiling, bricklaying, masonry, factory and warehouse
Indicative salary Approximately €600–€700 monthly for the general package
Employer support Accommodation and one meal may be provided
Permit route Employer-supported residence and work authorisation
Apply for Serbia Check Eligibility

⚡ Official single-permit procedure

A properly completed electronic single-permit request may be decided within 15 days. This official period begins after a complete request has been submitted and does not include recruitment, document preparation, Visa D or embassy waiting time.

📄 General candidate documents

CV, valid passport, employment or training evidence, police clearance, medical report, passport photograph, reference, signature and any employer-requested work video.

🛡️ Important protection

No job, work permit or visa is guaranteed. Final approval remains with the employer and the relevant Serbian authorities.

Before you apply: Job availability, salary, accommodation, nationality eligibility, tuition, service charges, embassy rules and processing periods can change. Every applicant must receive a current written offer and requirements before making a payment. TOPELA GLOBAL does not guarantee employment, admission, permits or visas.
